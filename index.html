About Trandy Construction

At Trandy Construction, we believe that a building is more than just concrete and steel—it's the foundation for your future. With over 30 years of expertise in Gampaha and across Sri Lanka, we specialize in delivering high-quality residential and commercial construction projects that stand the test of time.

From laying the first foundation stone to the final coat of paint, our team of seasoned engineers, architects, and skilled builders are dedicated to transforming your blueprints into reality. We pride ourselves on strict adherence to safety standards, timeline management, and structural integrity.

Discover the difference of working with a construction partner committed to transparency and excellence. We merge traditional durability with modern architectural trends to deliver spaces that are not just safe, but also aesthetically pleasing and functional.
