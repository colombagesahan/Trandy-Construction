Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you operate in besides Gampaha?

While we are based in Gampaha, Trandy Construction operates throughout the Western Province and surrounding areas. For large-scale commercial projects, we are available to work island-wide across Sri Lanka. Please contact us to discuss your specific location.

Do you handle council approvals and permits?

Yes, we provide end-to-end support. Our team assists with preparing architectural drawings, structural certificates, and submitting necessary documents to local councils (Pradeshiya Sabha/Urban Council) to obtain building permits and approvals smoothly.

Can you build on a "Labor Only" basis?

We offer flexible contract models. We can work on a "Labor Only" basis where you supply materials, or a "Turnkey" basis where we handle both materials and labor. We generally recommend Turnkey solutions as it allows us to control quality and timeline better.

How do you ensure the quality of materials used?

We source materials only from reputable suppliers and manufacturers in Sri Lanka. Whether it's cement, steel, or finishing items, we verify the quality against industry standards. We are also transparent with our clients and can provide material samples for approval.

How long does it take to build a standard two-story house?

The timeline depends on the size, design complexity, and weather conditions. Typically, a standard two-story residential project takes between 6 to 10 months to complete from foundation to handover. We provide a detailed schedule before starting any project.
